# Kinship 

Sanksrit text point at :

`Kula` -> Kins
`Jnati` (not `Jaati`)-> Kinfolk(Rishtedaar) 
`Vansha` -> Lineage

---

`Patriliny`: Tracing decendents from Father to son, grandson and so on.

`Matriliny`: Decendentas is traced through mother.

Our Hindu society strictly followed patriliny. In text like Mahabharat we get the message that only elder sons hold the righteous right to inherit.

`Kanyadan` (Gift of daughter) was and is considered on of the biggest duty of father.

In hindu society mother and daughters had no claim over property.

# Marriage 

`Endogamy`: Was done strictly in the caste the family belongs to but never in their kinship.

`Exogamy`: Marriage outside the caste or inside kinship was condemend. 

`Polyginy`: Males had more than 1 wife.
`Polyandry`: Mahabhrat gives refrence of woemn having more than 1 husband. (Draupadi)

# Gotra of a women

The system of gotra was maniky followed in Brahmanical society. 

Rules of Gotra:

1. Marriage can't happen in same gotra.
2. Women adopts husband's gotra after marriage.

Gotras named after -> Vedic seer(Rishi)

Satvahana queens used gotra of their father as it is. Sons adopted the gotra of mother in their name such as `Raja gotami putra shri satkarni`

Satvahanas along with refrences in Brihadaaranyak upnishad followed `Metronymics`.

`Metronymic`: Name derived from a person's mother instead of father. (Opposite: `Patronymics`: one being named after father.)

They didnt followed `metrilineal` system.

# Varna System 

Rigveda (Purusukta) gives :

1. Brahman.
2. Kshatriya.
3. Vaishya.
4. Shudra.

But soon they encountered other people in society they found that not everyone fits in this frame. So they introduced Jaati/Caste.

Golsmiths -> Suvarnakar.
Ironsmith -> Loahkar.
Venakar -> Basket maker.
Rathakar -> Chariot maker.

In a nutshell: Caste == Occupational groups.
Upper caste == Better occupation group.

## Inter caste marriage (Varna-sankar):

Male(m), Female(f)

1. Anulom vivah -> m(Higher caste) -> f(Lower caste)
2. Pratilom -> f(Higher caste) -> m(Lower caste)

Offsprings from `Pratilom vivah` == Untouchable.
For e.g Brahman(female) + Shudra(male) -> Chandal.

> Slowly intercaste marriages stopped.
