# Ibn Batuta's book: `Rihal`

## About `Ibn Batuta`: 

1. Born in a scholar Islamic family of Tangeria, Morocco.

2. Began his journey to India in `1322-1333`. Before that he had already been on `Pilgrimage` of `Mecca-Madiana` and along travel to most of `Middle east`.

3. Came to India through `Multan` in reign of `Muhammed Bin Tuglaq` (1325-1351).

4. Came to home in `1354`.

5. Was appointed as `Qazi/Judge` after impressing `Muhammed bin Tuglaq` through his scholarship. Remaind on this position for several years but was arrested. 

Soon he was sent to China as a messanger of `Muhammed bin Tuglaq`.

`Delhi` -> `Malabar` -> `Maldives` -> `Shrilanka` -> `Malabar` -> `Maldives` -> `Assam` -> `Arunanchal`  -> `Sumatra` -> `China`'s town named `Zaytun`(now Quanzhou)

He was the second person who had travlled `China` that much after `Marko Polo`.

6. `Ibn Juzzay` was the writer of `Rihal` who was put on the work by the `Ruler of Morocco`.

7. His accounts opened gates for multiple travellers.
