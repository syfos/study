# Changes that gave a rise to `Puranic Hinduism`.
Composition : 1st century bce
1. Everyone was allowed to read.
2. Simple sanskrit.
3. Indra, Agni, Som, Varun, Prajapati, Rudra and Vishnu were worshiped in Vedas and non vedic traditions had their own Gods like some worshipped `Boar`, `Lion` etc.

Brahmins told that these are the incarnations of our God and your are free to worship them in their form.

They crafted simple stories about these incarnations and that's how Hinduism started to take its modern form.

Even Shudras and Womens were allowed to read Purans.

-> The method of worshiping got softened and simpler.

-> From here came concept of `Bhakti` (personal devotion and love towards God).
